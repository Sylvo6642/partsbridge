# Demo walkthrough (planned; becomes real on Day 7)

What a visitor does, start to finish. About 5 minutes.

1. **Sign up** with email and password. You get a few sample requests from bot buyers to quote on.
2. **Buyer side:** paste a request such as:
   > Hi, need 10x 6205 sealed bearings and 4 belts A42 for the conveyor, plus one 1.5kW 4 pole motor foot mount. Urgent.
3. The AI shows the parts list it understood. Each line has a matched catalog part and a confidence score.
   If it is unsure, pick from its top suggestions.
4. **Publish.** Within a minute a bot seller sends a quote.
5. **Switch to Seller.** Open a request in your inbox and click **Draft with AI**. The AI fills in price, stock and
   lead time using tools. Check it, change anything, then submit.
6. **Switch back to Buyer.** Compare the quotes and accept one.
